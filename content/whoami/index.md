I'm a software engineer with 4+ years building distributed backend systems: REST microservices in **Go**, **Java**, and **Python**, designed for high availability and low latency. Before backend, I spent ~13 years in industrial automation and real-time systems — a different kind of engineering, but it's where I learned that failures are expensive and monitoring isn't optional.

## Work

Since 2024 I've been at **Mercado Livre**, building microservices for Proximity, its food-delivery business unit and one of its fastest-growing departments. There I design APIs for internal platforms and delivery partners, implement asynchronous processing over message queues, apply Clean Architecture and Domain-Driven Design, and monitor production on Fury, the company's internal developer platform. AI coding assistants are part of my daily workflow at this point, not a novelty.

## Background

I hold a bachelor's degree in **Control and Automation Engineering** (UNIP) and postgraduate specializations in **Advanced Go Development** (<a href="https://goexpert.fullcycle.com.br/pos-goexpert/" target="_blank">**Full Cycle**</a>) and **Java Architecture and Development** (<a href="https://postech.fiap.com.br/curso/arquitetura-desenvolvimento-java" target="_blank">**FIAP**</a>). Before software, I built real-time and computer-vision systems for industrial automation, working with clients like Mercedes-Benz. Portuguese is my native language, I'm fluent in English, and I speak intermediate Spanish.

Lately, my job title is Human-in-the-Loop: AI writes the code, I read the diff, nod twice, and pretend I'm still a software engineer.
